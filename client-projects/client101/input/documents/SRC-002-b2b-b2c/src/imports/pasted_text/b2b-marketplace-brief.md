For a B2B marketplace, the feature set should shift from simple retail shopping toward trade buying, quotation, bulk pricing, credit, repeat ordering, seller negotiation, and business-account workflows.

Your existing proposal already supports B2B/B2C pricing, multiple warehouses, ordering, payments, returns, ERP, and customer groups, so these are a good foundation for the B2B layer.

Features I Would Prioritize for Your Market

For a Tier-2 city B2B marketplace, these are especially important:

1. RFQ instead of only “Buy Now”

For a ₹500 tap, normal checkout works. For ₹1.5 lakh of sanitaryware or electrical products, buyers are likely to ask:

“Give your best price for this quantity.”

So each product/cart should support Buy Now and Request Quote.

2. Bulk Material List

A contractor could upload:

40 taps
12 basins
8 WC units
100 pipe fittings
15 fans

The marketplace should distribute the RFQ to suitable sellers and return quotations.

This can become one of the strongest B2B features.

3. Trade Price Tiers

Example:

Buyer	Price
Retail	₹1,250
Plumber / Contractor	₹1,170
Dealer	₹1,110
Distributor / Bulk	Quote

The proposal's existing customer-group and price-list capability is a useful base for this model.

4. Business Credit

Verified regular customers may have:

Credit Limit: ₹2,00,000
Available: ₹1,35,000
Payment Terms: 30 Days

This becomes particularly valuable for dealers, contractors and repeat institutional buyers.

5. Quick Reorder

B2B buyers do not necessarily want to browse every time.

Useful options:

Previous Order → Reorder

Suggested MVP

For the first B2B marketplace release, I would keep the customer-facing MVP to:

Business registration + GST details
Multi-category catalogue
Multi-seller products
Seller-wise price and inventory
MOQ and pack size
Quantity-based pricing
B2B/customer-specific pricing
RFQ / Request Best Price
Bulk enquiry
Multi-seller cart
GST checkout
UPI / bank / gateway / COD
Local delivery and pickup
Business order dashboard
Invoice and quotation downloads
Repeat order
WhatsApp assistance
Returns/claims
Credit purchase for approved customers

The strongest positioning is therefore not simply “B2B e-commerce.”

It could be positioned as a local B2B procurement marketplace where retailers, dealers, contractors and other business buyers can discover nearby suppliers, compare prices, request quotations, purchase in bulk, obtain credit and manage repeat procurement from one platform.
