D2C Marketplace — Feature Note
1. Product Positioning

The D2C marketplace is a consumer-facing multi-seller commerce platform designed for Tier-2 and emerging Indian markets.

It supports multiple product categories such as:

Bathroom and plumbing products
Stainless steel taps and fittings
Tiles and plywood
Electrical products including Orient / Orient Electric
Agriculture inputs and seeds
Pumps, machines and equipment
General construction and home-improvement products

The objective is to provide a familiar, trustworthy shopping experience while allowing multiple third-party sellers to participate on the same platform.

14. Marketplace Finance Behind D2C

Although customers don't see it, every marketplace order creates platform-level financial activity.

For each order:

Customer Payment

→ seller product value
→ marketplace commission
→ payment gateway fee
→ logistics adjustment
→ promotional contribution
→ seller payable

This feeds the shared Commerce OS + ERP backend.

So, for example:

Order #P100245

Customer pays ₹4,148.

Commerce OS can separately calculate:

Seller payable
Platform commission
Gateway charge
Promotion adjustment
Refund liability where applicable

This connects the D2C storefront directly with platform finance and seller settlement.

15. Core D2C User Journey

The primary flow should remain very simple:

Home

→ Search / Category

→ Product

→ Compare Sellers

→ Add to Cart

→ Smart Multi-Seller Cart

→ Checkout

→ UPI / Card / COD

→ Order Confirmation

→ Tracking

→ Return / Reorder

The customer should never need to understand the complexity of multiple sellers, warehouses, ERP or settlements behind the transaction.

Key Differentiators

The D2C experience becomes stronger if positioned around five ideas:

Differentiator	Value
Multi-category marketplace	One destination for home, construction, electrical and agriculture needs
Local + third-party sellers	Better availability and regional fulfilment
Seller comparison	Price, delivery and trust in one place
Tier-2-friendly commerce UX	UPI, COD, WhatsApp assistance and simple navigation
Integrated Commerce OS	Orders, stock, sellers, finance and settlements operate on one backend

The proposition is therefore not simply “an ecommerce website.”

It is a multi-seller D2C marketplace connected directly to inventory, fulfilment, platform finance and ERP operations, while keeping the customer journey simple and consumer-friendly
